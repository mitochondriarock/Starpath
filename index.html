Light bends when leaving water for air. A submerged fish appears shallower than its true position, so a straight-line practice spear aimed at the image will pass above it. This models optics, not fishing regulations or conditions.

Trial 01 / 03 · Refraction

Aim below the image.

A fish's apparent position is shown in pale blue. Adjust your aim below that image, then test your prediction. The actual fish stays hidden until you throw.

Physics briefingAt the surface, light follows n₁ sin θ₁ = n₂ sin θ₂. Water (n ≈ 1.333) bends outgoing rays away from the normal as they enter air (n ≈ 1.000).
